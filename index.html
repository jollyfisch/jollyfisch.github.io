About Me

Hi there!

Here is where I talk a little bit about myself... Well, after graduating from IIT Kharagpur with a degree in Computer Science and Engineering, I'm living the life of a software developer.

Enough about academic and professional life. When I'm not debating office commute vs. WFH, I keep myself occupied with books, music, mobile games and anime. Theatre used to see me on stage but since being a member of the workforce, plays are now for my eyes only. With some photos clicked during my occasional travels, I hope this page lets one to get to know me better.

About Hobbies...

A theatre enthusiast through and through, theatre has been part of my extra academic life in college. I became part of Druheen a dramatics club in my first year and I fell in love with theatre. We staged plays of some of the eminent playwrights - Manoj Mitra, Soumitra Chattopadhyay, Manmath Roy, Chandan Sen. In my final year we proudly staged D.L. Roy's "Sajahan". Even now, whenever I get the chance I find myself booking tickets to professional theatres.

Also a book lover, my interests lean towards English and Bangla literature. Thriller, horror, sci-fi... I enjoy all genres. And I try to write too, in my native tongue, Bangla. What's more fun than creating something of your own! I have written some poems and short stories. A lot of these works are collaborative, with my friend Aritra Mitra, a partner in crime for the innovative mischiefs we have committed in college. (I assure the scope of these mischiefs was strictly literary!)

As they say, Bengalis come pre-installed with the singing skill. I haven't received any formal training in singing or music. But in hostel life with with no one complain you get ample opportunity to sing your heart out. After graduation, I'm learning to play the Indian flute. It's a journey I wish to continue on.

I've been into mobile games since I was a kid. (And though my friends and coworkers encourage me to move to PC gaming, I'm trying my best not to go down that rabbit hole) Here's some that successfully stole my heart - Shadow Fight 2, Vector, Swordigo, Genshin Impact.

I've been into anime since my first year in college. Let's not talk about how many hours vanished into void. Looking for recommendations? Maybe I'll put a list in the next patch.

I love to travel. The list isn't that long... yet. I'll be definitely going places.
